# research copilot

Research Copilot is Sidera's market intelligence layer. It helps traders move from vague market curiosity to a structured, evidence-backed thesis.

Use Research Copilot when you need to:

* Scan markets for opportunities.
* Compare multiple assets.
* Understand catalysts.
* Review on-chain or derivatives context.
* Build a long or short thesis.
* Identify invalidation levels.
* Prepare a trading plan.

## What to ask

Good research prompts are specific. They define the market, time horizon, and decision you are trying to make.

### Market scan

```
Scan BTC, ETH, and SOL for the next 24-72 hours. Compare liquidity, trend strength, volatility, catalysts, and downside risk. Rank them by opportunity quality.
```

### Thesis development

```
Build both a long and short thesis for BTC. Include the logic, trigger conditions, invalidation levels, risk/reward, and the timeframe where the thesis is most relevant.
```

### Volatility expansion

```
Find crypto markets showing volatility expansion, unusual volume, or potential breakout behavior. Explain the trigger, key levels, and major risks.
```

### Risk and sizing

```
Design a position-sizing plan for a BTC trade. Include entry, stop, targets, per-trade risk percentage, position size, and risk/reward.
```

### Intraday monitoring

```
Act as an intraday monitoring assistant. List the market triggers I should track across price, volume, funding, news, and on-chain changes.
```

## Expected output

A professional research response should include:

| Section      | Purpose                                                                           |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Summary      | One-paragraph answer for quick decision-making.                                   |
| Evidence     | Data points, market structure, catalyst notes, and cited sources where available. |
| Bull case    | Conditions that support upside or long exposure.                                  |
| Bear case    | Conditions that support downside or risk-off behavior.                            |
| Trigger      | The event or price behavior that would activate the trade idea.                   |
| Invalidation | The level or condition that proves the thesis wrong.                              |
| Risk         | What can go wrong, including liquidity, leverage, news, and correlation risks.    |
| Next action  | Whether to watch, backtest, size, execute, or discard the idea.                   |

## Research quality checklist

Before acting on a research answer, verify:

* The timeframe matches your trading style.
* The source quality is sufficient.
* The thesis includes both upside and downside.
* The invalidation condition is explicit.
* The risk/reward is realistic after fees and slippage.
* The idea can be translated into executable rules.

## From research to strategy

Research Copilot should not be the final step. Once a thesis looks promising, convert it into a strategy request for Strategy Forge.

Example:

```
Turn this SOL long thesis into a rules-based strategy. Use 4-hour candles, define entry triggers, invalidation, stop-loss, take-profit logic, and maximum 1% account risk per trade.
```
