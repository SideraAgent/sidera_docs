# research prompts

Use research prompts to turn noisy market information into a structured thesis. The goal is to decide whether an idea deserves further strategy work, not to force a trade.

## Daily market scan

```
Scan BTC, ETH, SOL, and the highest-liquidity altcoin opportunities for the next 24-72 hours.

Compare:
- Liquidity
- Trend strength
- Volatility
- Catalysts
- Derivatives positioning
- On-chain signals
- Downside risk

Rank the opportunities from strongest to weakest. For each market, provide bull case, bear case, trigger, invalidation level, and recommended next action: reject, watch, backtest, or prepare execution.
```

## Single-asset thesis

```
Build a complete trading thesis for SOL over the next 3-7 days.

Include:
- Current market structure
- Bull case
- Bear case
- Key price levels
- Derivatives positioning
- On-chain or wallet activity if relevant
- Social or narrative momentum
- Invalidation level
- Main risks
- Confidence level

End with a decision: no trade, monitor, backtest, or prepare a strategy.
```

## Catalyst research

```
Analyze whether the current catalyst for [ASSET] is tradeable.

Separate:
- Confirmed facts
- Market rumors
- Weak signals
- Price reaction so far
- Liquidity conditions
- What would confirm continuation
- What would invalidate the trade

Do not recommend execution unless the catalyst has a clear trigger and invalidation level.
```

## Long versus short comparison

```
Build both a long and short thesis for [ASSET].

For each side, include:
- Entry trigger
- Invalidation
- Expected holding period
- Risk/reward
- Key data that supports the thesis
- Key data that contradicts the thesis

Then compare which side has better asymmetry and explain whether the best action is trade, wait, or reject.
```

## Narrative scan

```
Identify the strongest crypto market narratives this week.

For each narrative, include:
- Representative assets
- Evidence that attention is increasing
- Liquidity quality
- Whether the move is early, mid-cycle, or late
- Main risks
- Best way to express the view

Exclude illiquid assets and hype-only setups without clear invalidation.
```

## Market regime prompt

```
Classify the current market regime for BTC and ETH.

Use:
- Trend
- Volatility
- Volume
- Funding
- Open interest
- Liquidation risk
- Macro or news risk where relevant

Tell me which strategy types are suitable right now: breakout, momentum, mean reversion, range trading, hedge, or no trade.
```
