# Glossary

## Backtest

A historical simulation of strategy rules. It helps evaluate whether a strategy may be worth testing further.

## Drawdown

The decline from a portfolio or strategy peak to a later low. Drawdown is one of the most important risk metrics.

## Dry-run

A non-live operating mode where Sidera can simulate strategy actions and logs without placing real exchange orders.

## Invalidation

The condition that proves a trade thesis wrong.

## Leverage

Borrowed or synthetic exposure that increases both potential gains and losses.

## Liquidity

The ability to enter or exit a position without moving the market too much.

## Market regime

The current market environment, such as trending, ranging, high volatility, low volatility, risk-on, or risk-off.

## Profit factor

Gross profit divided by gross loss. A profit factor above 1 means historical gross wins exceeded historical gross losses.

## Risk budget

The amount of account equity or capital allocated to potential loss on a trade or strategy.

## RiskGuard

Sidera's deterministic rule engine that blocks orders violating hard risk limits.

## Risk Sentinel

Sidera's monitoring layer for positions, strategy state, alerts, and intervention workflows.

## Slippage

The difference between expected execution price and actual fill price.

## Smart Execution

Sidera's execution layer, responsible for pre-trade checks, routing, and execution logging.

## Strategy allocation

The portion of capital a strategy is allowed to use.

## Strategy Forge

Sidera's strategy creation layer that translates natural-language ideas into executable logic and backtests.

## Venue

An exchange or trading platform where orders are placed.
