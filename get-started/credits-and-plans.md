# Credits and plans

Sidera uses credits to meter AI-powered actions such as research, strategy generation, backtesting, and live execution workflows. The exact plan limits may change over time, but the operating principle is simple: the platform should show the estimated credit cost before a billable AI action runs.

## What credits power

Credits may be used for:

- AI research queries.
- Multi-agent market analysis.
- Strategy generation.
- Backtest runs.
- Strategy validation.
- Live execution workflows.
- Advanced monitoring and review features.

## Cost visibility

Before running a credit-consuming action, review:

- The estimated credit cost.
- The action being performed.
- The market or strategy involved.
- Whether the task is research-only, simulation, or live execution.
- Whether the result will trigger follow-up actions.

{% hint style="info" %}
For professional trading operations, treat credit usage like compute usage. Track it by workflow: research, strategy development, backtesting, execution, and monitoring.
{% endhint %}

## Plan selection

Choose a plan based on workflow intensity.

| User type | Typical needs |
| --- | --- |
| Research-only user | Market scans, AI summaries, thesis development, limited strategy exploration. |
| Active trader | Deeper research, strategy generation, standard backtesting, multiple active strategy tasks. |
| Power user or team | Higher-volume research, batch backtesting, more concurrent strategies, collaboration, advanced monitoring. |

## Managing usage

To keep usage predictable:

1. Start with focused prompts instead of broad open-ended research.
2. Reuse previous research context when possible.
3. Run smaller backtests before large batch tests.
4. Archive failed strategy ideas quickly.
5. Reserve live execution credits for strategies that have passed validation.
